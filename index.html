DeckJockey: Turntables & Mixer

Support · Open Civic Technology Ventures LLC

DeckJockey is a two-deck DJ app for iPhone and iPad: two turntables and a full mixer. On iPad in landscape both decks sit side by side with the mixer between them. On iPhone, or with iPad upright, you work one deck at a time while the other keeps playing. On iPhone Duo, unfold for a turntable on each half of the screen with the mixer on the hinge.

Contact

Common questions

Headphone cue: iPhone and iPad can't send separate audio to the built-in speaker and wired headphones at the same time, so cueing uses a split cable (master in one ear, cue in the other) or a USB-C audio interface with four outputs. Choose yours in Settings → Audio → Output.

A song shows "protected or not downloaded": iOS doesn't let third-party apps process DRM-protected Music songs or songs that aren't downloaded. Listen mode can still play them through the system music player.

Where are my recordings? In the Recordings sheet (the waveform button on the rail), and in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → DeckJockey → Recordings.
