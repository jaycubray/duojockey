DuoJockey: Turntables & Mixing

Support · Open Civic Technology Ventures LLC

DuoJockey is a two-deck DJ app made for iPhone Duo: unfold for two turntables with the mixer on the hinge, fold to keep mixing one deck while the other plays.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: support@open-civ.com. Please include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

Headphone cue: iPhone can't send separate audio to its speaker and wired headphones at the same time, so cueing uses a split cable (master in one ear, cue in the other) or a USB-C audio interface with four outputs. Choose yours in Settings → Audio → Output.

A song shows "protected or not downloaded": iOS doesn't let third-party apps process DRM-protected Music songs or songs that aren't downloaded. Listen mode can still play them through the system music player.

Where are my recordings? In the Recordings sheet (the waveform button on the rail), and in the Files app under On My iPhone → DuoJockey → Recordings.
