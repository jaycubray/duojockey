DuoJockey Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

DuoJockey does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Your music

Music you add from your Music library, from Files folders, or by importing files is processed only on your device, to play it and to detect its tempo and key. Analysis results, playlists, cue points and recordings are stored only on your device. If you use iCloud Drive folders, iOS may download those files from your own iCloud account.

Recordings

Recordings you make stay on your device until you choose to share them.
